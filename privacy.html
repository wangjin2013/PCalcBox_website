PCalcBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

PCalcBox is a privacy vault that keeps your photos, videos, audio and documents on your own device, protected by a real password and a decoy password. The app has no account and no sign-in.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or share your photos, videos, audio recordings, documents or passwords. Everything you add stays in the app's private storage on your device. Passwords are stored as salted hashes in the iOS Keychain and never leave the device.

The app itself uses no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no server of ours ever receives your content.

Permissions Explanation

The app asks for the following permissions to provide its features:

  • Photo Library — to import photos into the app and to save photos back to your library.
  • Microphone — to record audio inside the app.
  • Local Network — used by the built-in HTTP server, which lets a computer on the same Wi-Fi network browse and upload files. It listens on your local network only, and every request must be authenticated with your real privacy password.
  • App Tracking Transparency — used for advertising, described below.

Advertising

PCalcBox displays banner ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To request, deliver and measure ads, and to detect invalid traffic, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) when you allow tracking
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped
  • Diagnostic data related to ad delivery

On first launch the app asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you allow it, ads may be personalised using your advertising identifier. If you decline, the app still shows ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is presented before any ads are requested. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your files stay on your device until you delete them in the app. Deleting PCalcBox removes all of its stored content, including the passwords held in the Keychain. Because we never receive your content, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children's Privacy

PCalcBox is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age that applies in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top will be revised when we do.
